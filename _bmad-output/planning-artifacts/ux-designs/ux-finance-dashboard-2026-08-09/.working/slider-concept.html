Percentage Split Slider

Interactive concept for replacing form inputs with visual slider control

2-person split (1 handle)

50%
50%
Alice
Bob
Interaction: Drag the handle left/right to adjust percentages. Tap a segment to focus it.

3-person split (2 handles)

33%
33%
34%
Alice
Bob
Carol
Interaction: 2 handles give independent control. Last percentage (Carol) auto-calculated to reach 100%.

4-person split (3 handles)

25%
25%
25%
25%
Alice
Bob
Carol
Dave
Interaction: 3 handles distribute freely. Dave's percentage always = 100% - (Alice + Bob + Carol).

Key Properties

  • Handles: N-1 draggable handles for N people
  • Live feedback: Percentages update in real-time as you drag
  • Last person auto-calc: Always sums to 100% automatically
  • Tap to select: Tapping a segment highlights it (visual feedback)
  • Free-form: Any value 0–100 per person, no constraints
  • Responsive: Scales on mobile (taller touch target) and desktop
